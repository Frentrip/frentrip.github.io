Delete your Frentrip account

Frentrip is made by Matt Williams. This page explains how to delete your Frentrip account and what happens to your information when you do. You can do it from the app, or ask us by email if you no longer have the app.

In the app (the quickest way)

  1. Open Frentrip and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the page.
  4. Read what will happen, then confirm. If you signed in with Apple, your iPhone asks you to confirm with Apple once more.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Without the app

Send an email to williams.geospatial@gmail.com from the email address you sign in to Frentrip with, with the subject "Delete my Frentrip account". If you write from a different address, tell us the one you sign in with. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in: your email address, and the link to your Google or Apple account. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to remove Frentrip from your Apple ID's list of apps that use Sign in with Apple. If it still appears there, remove it in your Apple ID settings (Sign in with Apple).
  • Your profile: your name, username, photo, bio, home city and country, and date of birth.
  • Your friends list, friend requests, invite links you made, and the people you blocked.
  • The photos and files you uploaded.
  • The text of every message you sent, and your reactions to messages.
  • Your notifications, and the notifications other people got because of something you did.
  • Your notification tokens, so your phone gets no more notifications from us.
  • The scrambled copy of your email address that lets friends find you from their contacts.
  • Any trip that only you are on.

What is kept, and why

When a trip is shared with other people, some of what you added stays, so that the trip still makes sense for them. It is no longer linked to you: it shows as "Deleted user", with no name, photo or username.

  • Expenses and payments you were part of, so everyone else's balances still add up.
  • Plans, lists and polls you added to a shared trip, and your answers to polls.
  • Messages you sent appear as deleted, with their text removed.
  • Trips you own with other people pass to another member instead of being deleted.

How long it takes

Deleting from the app takes effect immediately. A request by email is handled within 30 days. Deleted information is removed from our backups within 30 days after that.

Questions

Write to williams.geospatial@gmail.com. Our privacy policy has the full details of what we collect and why.
