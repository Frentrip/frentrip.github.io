Frentrip Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 November 2026

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Frentrip is an app for planning trips with friends. This policy explains what information Frentrip collects, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it. We have tried to write it so you can read it in a few minutes.

"Frentrip", "we" and "us" mean Matt Williams, who runs the Frentrip apps for iOS, Android and the web. If you have a question, write to williams.geospatial@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your name, email, a username, the trips you plan, and the messages you send in them.
  • We do not sell your information. We do not show ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. There are no advertising or tracking tools in the app. When the app crashes or something goes wrong, it sends a technical report to Expo so we can fix it, without your name, email or messages.
  • If you use "Find friends from your contacts", your contacts' email addresses are scrambled on your phone before anything is sent, and we do not keep them.

What we collect, and why

When you sign in

You sign in with Google or, on iPhone, Sign in with Apple. We do not see or store a password. From your sign-in provider we receive:

  • Your name, used as your display name. You can see and use the app under it.
  • Your email address, used to identify your account and, unless you turn it off, to let people who already have your email find you (see "Finding friends" below). If you choose Apple's Hide My Email, we receive a private relay address instead of your real one, and that relay address is the only one we ever know.
  • Your profile picture link, if Google provides one, shown next to your name. Apple does not provide one.
  • An account ID from the provider, so the same account signs you in next time.

We also give your account an internal user ID.

What you create in the app

  • Your username, which other people can see and search for.
  • Your default currency, if you choose one, used for new expenses and to show amounts converted into it. Only you can see it.
  • Trips: their names and who is in them, and the plans, lists and other details you or your fellow travellers add.
  • Expenses: what was spent, who paid, how each cost is split, and payments between members to settle up.
  • Polls: the questions and options you add, and your votes.
  • Friends: who you are friends with, friend requests, invite links you create, and anyone you have blocked.
  • Messages you send in trip chats, threads and direct messages.

This is used only to run the app for you and the people you share trips with. Messages and trip details are visible to the other members of that trip or conversation, and to nobody else.

Other signed-in users can see your display name, username and profile picture, so that they can find you and recognise you in a trip. They cannot see your email address.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your phone gives the app a push token, an address for sending notifications to that device. We store it with your account, along with which platform it is (iOS, Android or web), so we can tell you about new messages, friend requests and trip invitations. We also keep a list of your recent notifications so the app can show what you have not read yet. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Finding friends from your contacts

This only happens if you tap "Find friends from your contacts" and then allow access when your phone asks. It never happens in the background, and the app works fine without it. It is not offered on the web.

When you use it:

  1. On your phone, the app reads the email addresses in your contacts. Nothing else from your address book is read.
  2. Still on your phone, each address is turned into a hash: a fixed string of characters computed from the address, which cannot simply be turned back into it. Only these hashes are sent to us.
  3. Our server compares them with hashes of the email addresses of people already on Frentrip and tells you which of your contacts are here.
  4. We do not store the hashes you send, or anything else from your address book. We keep only a count of how many you checked each day, so we can limit how many anyone can check (this stops people testing huge lists of addresses to learn who uses Frentrip). These counts are linked to your account.

A hash is not the same as anonymity: anyone who already knows a particular email address could check whether it belongs to someone on Frentrip. That is why the limits exist, and why you can opt out.

Being found. By default, someone who has your email address in their contacts can find you this way. To stop it, turn off "Let people find me by email" on your profile. When it is off we delete the hash of your email, so you cannot be matched at all.

Crash and error reports

When the app crashes, or something fails that should not (a screen that will not load, a notification that cannot be set up), the app sends a report to Expo, the company whose tools Frentrip is built with, so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

  • what went wrong: the error message and where in the app's code it happened
  • which kind of screen you were on (for example "a trip's messages", not which trip), how long screens took to load, and short technical notes about what the app was doing just before
  • your device model, operating system, app version and language setting
  • a random ID created when the app was installed, so we can tell whether one problem keeps happening on the same device. It is not linked to your account.

Reports do not include your name, email address, username, messages, search text or anything else you typed. They are used only to keep the app working, never for advertising or to build a profile of you.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, your photos, your phone number, payment information, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising or tracking tools.

Technical logs

Like any online service, the servers that run Frentrip record basic technical information about each request, such as IP address, time and the kind of device or browser. These logs are used to keep the service running and secure, and are deleted automatically after a short period.

Who else handles your information

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers who process it on our behalf, only to run Frentrip:

Provider What it does for us
Supabase Hosts our database and sign-in system, and stores everything above
Expo (650 Industries) Delivers app updates, relays push notifications, and receives crash and error reports
Apple (Push Notification service) Delivers notifications to iPhones
Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) Delivers notifications to Android phones
Google and Apple sign-in Confirm who you are when you sign in

A notification passes through Expo and Apple or Google on its way to your phone, so they see its text (for example, the start of a new message).

Your information is stored on Supabase's servers, which may be in the United States or other countries, and our other providers may also process it outside the country where you live. Where the law requires it, they do so under appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

We may disclose information if the law requires us to, or to protect people's safety or Frentrip from abuse.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. Push tokens are dropped when a device stops accepting notifications. Daily contact-matching counts are only needed for the day they cover.

Deleting your account. You can delete your account from your profile in the app. When you do, we delete your account, your profile, your friends list and friend requests, your blocks, your push tokens, your notifications and the hash of your email address. We also erase the text of every message you sent, delete the reactions you added to messages, and delete any photos you uploaded (the app does not offer photo uploads yet). Other things you added to a trip you shared with other people, such as plans and expenses, stay with that trip so the other members do not lose them, but they are no longer linked to your name. Deleted information is removed from our backups within 30 days. Our account deletion page has the steps, including how to ask by email if you no longer have the app.

Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Change your username in the app.
  • Edit or remove what you add to a trip in the app: change or delete plans and polls, and cancel expenses and payments. Messages cannot be edited or deleted yet.
  • Stop being findable by email, with "Let people find me by email".
  • Turn off notifications or contacts access in your phone's settings.
  • Block anyone, so they cannot find or contact you.
  • Delete your whole account, in the app or by email from the address you sign in with (see above).
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by writing to williams.geospatial@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), the law may give you further rights, such as objecting to how we use your information or complaining to your data protection authority. We will honour them. We use your information because it is needed to provide the service you asked for, and for our legitimate interest in keeping it safe and free of abuse.

Children

Frentrip is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Your information is sent over encrypted connections. Access is controlled so each person can read only what they are allowed to see, such as the trips they are a member of. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and list the change here. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
